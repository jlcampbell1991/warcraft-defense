Calvin’s Thoughts on Revelation 21

Calvin sees Revelation 21 as the consummate promise of God’s redemptive plan: a new heaven and a new earth where the former things have passed away. He emphasizes the holy city, the New Jerusalem, as the perfect fulfillment of God’s covenant—a place where God dwells with His people, wiping away every tear, and where there is no more death, mourning, crying, or pain.
