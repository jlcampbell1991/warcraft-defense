# Jensen Huang Apologizes to Employees and Says He’s in Therapy

During an all‑hands meeting at Nvidia, CEO Jensen Huang opened the session by acknowledging recent internal tensions and offering a sincere apology for any anxiety caused by his brief absence.

He disclosed that he has begun attending therapy each day, emphasizing the importance of mental‑health care for both himself and the broader company culture. Huang framed the decision as a personal commitment to well‑being and a signal that seeking help is acceptable in a high‑performance environment.

To keep communication transparent, Huang announced that leadership will host monthly feedback sessions where employees can directly voice concerns, ideas, and suggestions. These forums are intended to foster ongoing dialogue and ensure that staff feel heard throughout the organization.

Reactions among the team were mixed but largely supportive; many expressed relief at the openness and appreciated the new avenues for dialogue. Others were curious about the logistics of the therapy schedule and the structure of the feedback meetings.

Huang concluded by reaffirming his dedication to Nvidia’s product roadmap while also prioritizing the health and morale of the workforce.
