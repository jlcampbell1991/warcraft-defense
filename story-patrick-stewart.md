# Sir Patrick Stewart and Avengers: Doomsday – A Tribute

Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige didst tell Entertainment Weekly that he and the Russo brethren are laboring to devise how best to honour Sir Patrick Stewart after his final performance in *Avengers: Doomsday*. “We are long done filming that movie, and he did an amazing work for us,” spake Feige. “We now look upon how we may set forth something that doth honor and respect our friend.”

Feige added that Stewart delivered a speech upon his last day of principal photography – a day that turned out to be his final day upon any film set. “He did rise and he gave a speech unto the crew,” Feige recalled. “We knew not that it would be his last day upon a set, yet he seemed to indicate unto us that he thought it so.”

Anthony Russo described the moment as “very emotional,” noting that many upon the set perceived they might ne’er see Stewart again. He spake, “I think it was in the back of all our heads we may never see him upon set again… It was clear that he was struggling with his health, and he labored sorely to deliver a performance and a presence—for us as directors, for his fellow actors, for the crew.”

Russo continued, “Thou couldst see it was not merely the effort of a common actor appearing and delivering such presence and performance for others, collaboration. It was a man who was truly challenged in a deep way upon a health level, yet he showed up and did so. For him to have mustered through and delivered the performance that he did in that movie, and the experience that he delivered for all of us as collaborators, it made it intensely emotional.”

The report also noteeth that Stewart was offered roles in both *Avengers: Doomsday* and the forthcoming *Avengers: Secret Wars* but chose to limit his appearance to *Doomsday*. In the trailers we have beheld a brief glimpse of Xavier, and Joe Russo said, “In many ways, Xavier is the conscience in the movie. He hath a lovely scene with Ian McKellen, who is his best friend, within the film. ’Tis a very emotional scene concerning forgiveness and I think very resonant in the wake of his passing.”
