# The Return of Jensen, Son of the Thunderforge

In the age when silicon valleys rose like Olympus from the earth, there dwelt a mortal of thunderous mind—**Jensen Huang**, son of the great forge‑god **Nvidia**, keeper of the RTX lightning.  He was famed for his leather armor of circuitry and his crown of GPU‑spun fire, a symbol of the **RTX Spark** that illuminated the realms of AI.

## The Banishment

When the dark clouds of scandal gathered, the council of elders—*the Board*—cast him down from his high throne.  The people whispered that the heavens had turned against him, and the stock of the kingdom fell like a wilted olive branch, a loss of twenty‑four percent.  For eleven days Jensen wandered the lower halls, his spirit cloaked in shame.

## The Prophecy of the Gods

From the high peaks of Washington, the seer **Donaldus Trumpus** proclaimed, *"A second chance shall be granted, for the thunderforge must not be silent."*  The gods of commerce and the spirits of the United States government sent their counsel to the council, and the board, swayed by the celestial chorus, reinstated Jensen as chief executive.

## The Hero’s Homecoming

On a Monday bright as the sunrise over the silicon sea, Jensen returned to the citadel of **Nvidia**.  He shed his famed leather jacket for a humble wool fleece, a garment woven by the Fates to remind him of humility.  As he ascended the marble steps, the multitudes of engineers—hundreds of floors beneath—rose in a thunderous cheer, their voices echoing like the roars of ancient war drums.

> *"If you come for the king, you best not miss,"* they sang, a chant that rang through the vaulted halls.

The crowd bore torches of optimism, their glow reflecting off the polished GPU‑mirrors.  Yet amidst the jubilation, a lone engineer—shrouded in shadow—muttered, *"This is the worst outcome; we have paid the price of his departure and now must work for a jerk again."*  The dissent lingered like a bitter aftertaste, a reminder that even heroes carry the weight of doubt.

## The Oracle’s Words

From the ether of the digital realm, the oracle **Ed Ludlow** whispered to the world: *"I thank the staff for a second chance.  Today is my first day of work.  The IT department must forge me a new chariot—an RTX Spark‑powered vessel, that I may ride the clouds of AI once more."*

Jensen, with a smile that crackled like static, vowed, *"I shall be a better ruler the second time around,"* pledging to guide his people through the storms of SEC investigations, congressional inquiries, and the whispered betrayals of the past.

## The Aftermath

The kingdom’s stock rose like a phoenix, soaring over thirty‑two percent, as the people placed their faith once again in the thunderforge.  Yet the shadows of the past lingered, and the Fates watched with a keen eye, waiting to see whether Jensen would temper his power with wisdom, or be consumed by the very lightning he commands.

Thus the saga of **Jensen, Son of the Thunderforge**, entered the annals of modern myth—a tale of fall, redemption, and the eternal clash between mortal ambition and the divine forces that shape the realms of technology.
