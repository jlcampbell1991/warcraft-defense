Calvin’s Thoughts on Revelation 20

Calvin interprets Revelation 20 as describing the thousand‑year reign of Christ, the final judgment, and the ultimate defeat of Satan, emphasizing the hope of eternal peace for the faithful.

He further notes that the binding of Satan for a thousand years signifies a period of restrained evil, allowing the saints to reign with Christ, and that the final release and defeat of Satan confirm God's ultimate victory and the establishment of a new heaven and earth.
