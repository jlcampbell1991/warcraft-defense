Warcraft Defense

Defend your castle from waves of invading units. Click to place towers, upgrade them, and watch the battle unfold. Survive as long as you can!

How to Play

  • Click on the map to place a tower at the desired location.
  • Each tower automatically attacks the nearest enemy within range.
  • Earn gold by destroying enemies; use gold to build more towers or upgrade existing ones.
  • Upgrade towers to increase damage, range, or firing speed.
  • Prevent enemies from reaching your castle; if they do, you lose health.

Rules

  • The game ends when the castle’s health drops to zero.
  • Waves become progressively harder with faster and tougher enemies.
  • Some towers have special abilities; read their tooltips for details.
  • There is no limit to the number of towers you can build, constrained only by your gold.

Calvin’s Thoughts on Matthew 24

Calvin reflects that Matthew 24 discusses the signs of the end times, urging believers to stay vigilant, watch for false prophets, and remain faithful as Christ returns. He emphasizes preparedness and the importance of living in accordance with God’s kingdom.

Calvin’s Thoughts on John 1

Calvin reflects that John 1 introduces the Word who was with God and is God, emphasizing the incarnation of Christ as the light that shines in the darkness, offering hope and salvation.

Calvin’s Thoughts on Revelation 20

Calvin interprets Revelation 20 as describing the thousand‑year reign of Christ, the final judgment, and the ultimate defeat of Satan, emphasizing the hope of eternal peace for the faithful.
