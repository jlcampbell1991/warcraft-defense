Warcraft Defense

Defend your castle from waves of invading units. Click to place towers, upgrade them, and watch the battle unfold. Survive as long as you can!

How to Play

  • Click on the map to place a tower at the desired location.
  • Each tower automatically attacks the nearest enemy within range.
  • Earn gold by destroying enemies; use gold to build more towers or upgrade existing ones.
  • Upgrade towers to increase damage, range, or firing speed.
  • Prevent enemies from reaching your castle; if they do, you lose health.

Rules

  • The game ends when the castle’s health drops to zero.
  • Waves become progressively harder with faster and tougher enemies.
  • Some towers have special abilities; read their tooltips for details.
  • There is no limit to the number of towers you can build, constrained only by your gold.
