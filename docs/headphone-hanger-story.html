Under‑Desk Headphone Hanger with Three 20 W Charging Ports

Multi‑use devices that pull together disparate features often turn out to be junk. But it’s hard to knock a $9.89 device that combines an under‑the‑desk headphone hanger, cable holder, and a three‑port 20 W PD USB charging hub.

Cozoo’s device is basically a compact three‑port charging brick with little wings for hanging headphones while they charge. It ships with both 3 M tape and screws for mounting under your desk, plus cable tie‑downs to keep the setup tidy.

It’s been stuck under my treadmill desk for three years using the 3 M tape option and shows no signs of falling off. At this price (and with a brand you’ve never heard of) you wouldn’t expect top‑notch audio circuitry, yet it has performed flawlessly for me.
