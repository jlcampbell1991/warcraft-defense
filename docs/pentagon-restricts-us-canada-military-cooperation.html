Pentagon restricts US‑Canada military cooperation – WaPo

The Washington Post reports that the Pentagon has placed new limits on joint military activities with Canada, citing concerns over security protocols and strategic alignment.

Key points include:

  • Reduced joint training exercises.
  • Enhanced vetting of Canadian personnel involved in U.S. programs.
  • Potential impact on existing defense agreements.
