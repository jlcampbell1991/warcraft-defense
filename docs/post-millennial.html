The Post Millennial

What Is The Post Millennial?

The Post Millennial is a digital news outlet that publishes conservative‑leaning commentary, opinion pieces, and news summaries. It markets itself as a “center‑right” alternative to mainstream media, focusing on politics, culture, and economics.

History & Growth

Founded in 2017 by Canadian journalist Josh Homan, the site quickly grew through social‑media distribution, reaching several million monthly visitors by 2022. It is owned by the media company Post Millennial Media Inc., which also operates a network of affiliated newsletters.

Typical Content & Style

Articles cover topics such as Canadian and U.S. politics, cultural trends, and economic policy. The tone is often informal and punchy, using headline‑style framing and occasional humor. Content is primarily text‑based with occasional images, videos, and embedded social‑media posts.

Criticism & Controversy

Fact‑checking organisations have flagged a number of stories for factual inaccuracies or misleading framing. Critics argue that the outlet blurs the line between opinion and news, sometimes presenting partisan commentary without clear labeling.

Conclusion

Despite ongoing criticism, The Post Millennial has become a notable voice in the online conservative ecosystem, influencing public discourse on social platforms and shaping the narrative around right‑leaning issues.
