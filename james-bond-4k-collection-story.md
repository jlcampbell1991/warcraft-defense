# James Bond 4K Ultra‑HD Collection – Shelf‑Space & Wallet Impact

After Robert Pattinson was officially announced as the new 007, the conversation quickly moved from *who* to *how* – how many shelves will survive the onslaught of 25 glossy 4K Blu‑ray cases, and how deep the pockets of a true Bond aficionado must be.

> "@VHSDVDBLURAY4K" – the tweet that set the numbers rolling.

## The numbers
- **Cases:** 25 × standard Blu‑ray box (≈ 14 cm × 19 cm × 1.5 cm) → roughly a **30 cm‑wide** wall of Bond, about the width of a typical TV stand.
- **Cost:** Assuming $45 per 4K disc, the set totals **$1,125** (plus taxes). Add a few dollars for a premium collector’s slipcase and you’re looking at a six‑figure‑ish *bond*.
- **Weight:** Approx. 0.2 kg per case → **5 kg** of pure spy‑gear, enough to tip a coffee table.
- **Shelf‑space:** If you stack the cases side‑by‑side, you need a **~30 cm** horizontal strip; stacked in two rows, you double the height to about **3 cm** per layer, still manageable on a sturdy bookshelf.

## Why it matters
Owning the complete collection turns any living‑room into a miniature MI6 armory. It’s a conversation starter, a financial commitment, and a test of one’s dedication to the franchise. The set also doubles as a *very* impressive display of loyalty to the new 007, especially when you can point out that each disc contains the full‑frame 4K restoration of classics like *Goldfinger* and the upcoming *Bond 25* starring Pattinson.

## The humor in the math
Imagine explaining to a friend: “I spent $1,125 on a 25‑disc set, and now my bookshelf is 30 cm wider than my couch.” The irony is that the *real* Bond would probably have a gadget to shrink the cases, but we’re stuck with physical media.

Will you have the shelf‑space, or will you have to sell a few pieces of furniture? The choice, dear reader, is yours.
